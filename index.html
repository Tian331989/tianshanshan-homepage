Professional Profile

个人简介

Research Focus

Dr. Shanshan Tian earned her Ph.D. in Psychiatry & Mental Health from Peking University under the supervision of Prof. Lin Lu. Her research spans the neuropharmacology of mental disorders, with particular emphasis on antidepressant drug development and clinical trial methodology.

She has pioneered frameworks for reducing placebo effects in novel antidepressant trials and authored China's comprehensive progress report on new antidepressant agents. Her work integrates molecular pharmacology, clinical trial design, and real-world evidence to advance psychiatric care.

研究方向

田姗姗博士于北京大学获得精神病与精神卫生学博士学位，师从陆林院士。研究方向涵盖精神疾病神经药理学，专注于抗抑郁药物研发与临床试验方法学。

在新型抗抑郁药临床试验的安慰剂效应降低策略方面开展了开创性工作，牵头撰写中国新型抗抑郁药进展报告。研究融合分子药理学、临床试验设计与真实世界证据，推动精神疾病诊疗进步。
